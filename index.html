How it works
  • 1 Customer submits the inquiry form.
  • 2 Lead Analyst agent scores the inquiry, assigns a specialist, and generates talking points.
  • 3 Communications Specialist agent writes a personalized follow-up email and sends it.
  • 4 Lead appears in the dashboard in near real time for the specialist to review.
  • 5 Specialist marks the lead as converted — triggering an automated post-sale email sequence.
